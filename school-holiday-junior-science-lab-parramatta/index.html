School Holiday: Junior Science Lab – Parramatta Library

Little scientists get to mix, bubble and explode their way through a series of hands-on experiments designed just for kids in this free school holiday workshop.

Parramatta City Library's Junior Science Lab is a favourite on the school holiday calendar. Children aged 5–10 put on lab coats and explore chemistry and biology through safe, spectacular experiments led by a science educator.

What's included:

  • Ages 5–10 years
  • Free — bookings essential
  • Lab coats and goggles provided
  • Experiments include volcanoes, slime and colour-changing liquids
  • Multiple sessions each week of the holidays
