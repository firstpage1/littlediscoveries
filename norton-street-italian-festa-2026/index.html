Norton Street Italian Festa 2026

Ciao, Sydney! Leichhardt's famous Italian street festival celebrates 40 years with a full day of music, performances, family fun, food and community spirit.

Why families will love it

The heart of Leichhardt becomes a lively Italian street celebration, with something to see, taste and do around every corner. Bring the family for street entertainment, cooking demonstrations, and the atmosphere of Sydney's Little Italy.

Highlights for the kids

  • Carnival fun: jumping castles, rides and dedicated kids' entertainment.
  • Animals and activities: an animal farm, pony rides, face painting and roaming street performers are advertised by the organiser.
  • Live music and dancing: enjoy stage entertainment throughout the day.
  • Italian cooking: free demonstrations from chefs and local nonnas.
  • Auto Festa: admire classic Italian cars and other vehicles.
  • Food stalls: sample or purchase pizza, pasta, gelato, coffee and much more.

Little Discoveries tip: Arrive near 10am to explore with little ones before the biggest crowds. Bring water, hats and comfy shoes; expect a busy outdoor festival.

The street festival is free to enter. Food, drinks, carnival rides, shopping and selected activities may cost extra. Individual attractions and performance times can change.

Getting there

Public transport is recommended. The L1 light rail to Marion or Leichhardt North is a good option. Major road closures and parking restrictions apply.

Norton Street is closed to vehicles during the event. Check Transport for NSW before leaving. Replacement buses may operate on the Inner West rail line.
