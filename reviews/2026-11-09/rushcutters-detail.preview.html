Rushcutters Bay Park Youth Recreation Area Opening

A brand-new place for young skaters, scooter riders and basketball fans is opening at Rushcutters Bay Park. Celebrate with live demonstrations, meet professional skateboarders and enjoy a free community morning.

About this activity

After months of work, Woollahra Council is opening a dedicated youth recreation area in Rushcutters Bay Park. Its new junior scooter and skate space is designed for beginner and intermediate riders, alongside a mini basketball and netball practice court. The opening morning gives local families a chance to explore this addition to the park together.

The free community celebration will include skateboarding and basketball demonstrations, opportunities to meet professional skateboarders, live music, and a free barbecue and gelato. Council is also promoting a draw to win a skateboard, helmet and lessons.

Why it’s worth going

  • A new place to play: explore a specially designed scooter, skate and ball-sports area for young people.
  • Watch skilled riders: skateboarding and basketball demonstrations, plus a chance to meet professional skateboarders.
  • Celebrate together: live music, a free barbecue and gelato make this more than a routine park visit.
  • Try somewhere new: the broader harbourside park also has lawns, a separate playground and foreshore paths.

Good to know

Free entry, but RSVP requested: Woollahra Council asks visitors to register their interest on its event page. This does not mean individual activities or giveaways are guaranteed. The new play area is specifically designed for young people aged 8–14, although the opening celebration welcomes everyone. Families with younger children can also use the existing playground.

Planning your visit

The event takes place on Saturday 14 November 2026, from 10am to midday, in Rushcutters Bay Park on New South Head Road, Edgecliff. The park has public toilets, an accessible toilet, a baby-change table and nearby transport connections. Street parking is time-limited. Check council updates and registration details before travelling, especially if the weather is poor.
