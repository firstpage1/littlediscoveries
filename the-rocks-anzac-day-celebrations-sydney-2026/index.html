The Rocks ANZAC Day – Free Live Music, Marching Bands & More

The Rocks hosts a full free ANZAC Day program on Saturday 25 April — live music, military marching bands, art exhibitions and a hearty diggers breakfast in one of Sydney's most historic precincts.

About this activity

The Rocks is Sydney's most historic neighbourhood — and on ANZAC Day it comes alive with a free program of events that honour those who served while creating a genuine community celebration. On Saturday 25 April 2026, the cobblestone lanes and sandstone buildings of The Rocks set the perfect backdrop for one of the city's most atmospheric ANZAC Day experiences.

The day's program includes live music performances, military marching bands winding through the historic streets, an ANZAC-themed art exhibition, and the beloved Diggers Breakfast — a hearty spread that continues the tradition of post-dawn service community gatherings. It's a wonderful way to spend ANZAC Day morning after the march or before the afternoon memorial tours.

Families can look forward to:

  • Free live music performances throughout the day across The Rocks precinct
  • Military marching bands performing through the historic laneways and squares
  • An ANZAC-themed art exhibition celebrating the stories of those who served
  • The iconic Diggers Breakfast — perfect for families who've been up since dawn
  • Historic sandstone buildings and cobblestone streets create a unique atmosphere
  • Steps from Circular Quay for ferries, trains and scenic harbour walks
  • Combine with a visit to the Museum of Contemporary Art (free general entry always)
