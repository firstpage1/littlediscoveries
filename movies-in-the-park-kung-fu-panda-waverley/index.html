About this activity

Pack your picnic blanket and head to Waverley Park for a free outdoor screening of Kung Fu Panda 4 — part of Waverley Council's 2026 Movies in the Park series.

Arrive early to grab a good spot on the grass as the park fills up fast. Credits roll at dusk.

  • Free entry — no bookings required
  • Free popcorn provided
  • BYO picnic, blankets, and low chairs
  • Family-friendly, all-ages atmosphere
  • Set in the heart of Waverley, Eastern Suburbs

One of Sydney's best free family traditions under the stars.
