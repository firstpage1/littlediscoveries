About this activity

Telstra Free Days at the MCA are one of Sydney's great cultural gifts — and Wednesday 15 April is your chance to experience one of the world's finest contemporary art museums without spending a cent.

Situated right on Circular Quay with views of the Opera House and Harbour Bridge, the MCA houses rotating exhibitions by leading Australian and international artists. On Free Days, the museum's family programs are also open, making this a brilliant school holiday outing for curious kids and art-loving parents alike.

Families can look forward to:

  • Free entry to all current MCA exhibitions — no ticket required
  • Family art trails and activity sheets available at the front desk
  • Drop-in kids' art-making activities in the learning studios
  • The MCA Café for lunch with Circular Quay harbour views
  • Gift shop with unique Australian art-inspired gifts
  • Easy access via Circular Quay train, ferry or bus
