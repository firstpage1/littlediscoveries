About this activity

Young makers aged 5–15 explore relief collage and recycled-material sculpture with the library’s Art Club. The animal theme includes experimenting with texture and making shaggy animal hair.

Why it’s worth going

  • Explore animals from the collection.
  • Build a relief collage.
  • Shape recycled materials into sculpture.

Good to know

The session is free and bookings are essential. It is specifically for ages 5–15.

Planning your visit

The workshop is in Stanton Library’s Level 2 Conference Room, 234 Miller Street, North Sydney. Lift access is available for people using mobility aids.
