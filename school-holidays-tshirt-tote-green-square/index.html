About this activity

Get creative and go green at this hands-on printmaking workshop for children aged 8–12 at Green Square Library.

Participants design, carve, and ink up their own custom block, then press it onto a pre-loved t-shirt or tote bag to take home — a unique wearable they've made themselves.

The workshop celebrates sustainability and upcycling, giving second-hand fabric a new life through art.

Parents or guardians must sign in and remain within the library space for the duration of the session. Bookings are essential — spaces are limited.
