About This Activity

Get ready for science that bubbles, fizzes and glows! These school holiday workshops at Ashfield Mall let kids roll up their sleeves and dive into hands-on experiments they can actually take home.

What Kids Will Do

  • Make their own bath bombs
  • Create slime and perfumes
  • Build a lava lamp
  • Explore chemical reactions in a fun, safe way

Good to Know

  • Suitable for ages 4 and over
  • Bookings are essential — sessions sell out fast
  • Located inside Ashfield Mall, opposite Kmart
  • Running across two school holiday weeks: 10–12 April and 15–17 April
