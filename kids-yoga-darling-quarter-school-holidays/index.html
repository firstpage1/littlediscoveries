About this activity

Start the morning right with free kids' yoga sessions at Darling Quarter every weekday from 13–17 April. These gentle, playful sessions are run by a qualified instructor and designed to make yoga fun and accessible for young children.

Set on the lush lawn at Darling Quarter — right next to the Darling Quarter playground, one of Sydney's best — these sessions combine simple stretches, breathing games, animal poses and mindful moments. It's the perfect morning activity before a big play on the adjacent equipment or a stroll around Darling Harbour.

Why it’s worth going

  • Free 45-minute kids' yoga sessions — no booking required
  • Qualified instructor leading fun, animal-themed yoga sequences
  • Suitable for ages 3 and up, parents welcome to join in
  • Darling Quarter playground right next door for post-yoga play
  • Easy access from Town Hall or Darling Harbour ferry
  • Combine with breakfast at one of the nearby Darling Quarter cafes
