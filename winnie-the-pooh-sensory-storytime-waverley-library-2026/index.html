Waverley Library is marking 100 years of Winnie-the-Pooh with a special free sensory storytime on Wednesday 20 May from 10:30am. It's designed for children aged 3–5 and combines picture book stories with hands-on sensory play — including craft and slime.

The session is part of Waverley Library's popular children's program, with the Winnie-the-Pooh theme giving it a special, one-off feel. It's a lovely midweek activity before Vivid Sydney kicks off on Friday.

Bookings are required and spaces are limited — register via Humanitix to secure your spot.

What's included:

  • Winnie-the-Pooh themed stories and read-alouds
  • Hands-on craft and sensory play activities
  • Slime making
  • One-off special event celebrating 100 years of Winnie-the-Pooh
  • Ages 3–5 years
  • Free — bookings essential via Humanitix
