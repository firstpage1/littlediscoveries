Pound Paws Dog Day at Bondi Beach

Calling all dog-loving families! Enjoy a lively Sunday morning at Bondi Pavilion with rescue pups, a pet market, live music and fun dog competitions, all in support of pet adoption.

What makes this one special?

Pound Paws is bringing the community together for a beachside celebration of our four-legged friends. Families can meet rescue dogs looking for loving homes, cheer on talented and best-dressed pups and browse boutique pet stalls.

What to look forward to

  • Meet the rescue dogs: chat to local rescue groups and learn about pet adoption.
  • Best Dog Trick & Best Dressed: enjoy the fun competitions and prizes.
  • Pup Pilates: dog-friendly exercise presented by Tavo Pets; check with the organiser for session access.
  • Live acoustic music: enjoy the atmosphere around Surfish Café, with dog-friendly seating.
  • Boutique pet market: browse treats, accessories and doggy essentials.

Little Discoveries tip: It’s a fun, relaxed outing for animal-loving kids, even without a dog of your own. Keep children close around unfamiliar animals, ask owners before patting and follow local dog-access rules.

The event itself is listed as free. Food, shopping or optional activities may cost extra. Free registrations may fill up, so check ticket availability before travelling.
