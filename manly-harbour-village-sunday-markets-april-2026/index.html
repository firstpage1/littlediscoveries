Every Sunday, Little Bayside Park in Manly comes alive with markets that have become a beloved institution on the Northern Beaches. Set right on the harbour with views across to the city, it's one of Sydney's most scenic market settings — and entry is completely free. Local artisans, fresh produce growers, food vendors and makers all set up stalls, and live music drifts through the park from mid-morning.

For families, the Manly Harbour Village markets are an easy, relaxed way to spend a Sunday morning. Kids can explore the stalls while parents browse, and the harbour foreshore is right there for a walk or a play. Combine it with a swim at Manly Beach (a short walk away) or breakfast at one of the many cafés along the Corso for a full day out on the Northern Beaches.

Families can look forward to:

  • Free entry to one of Sydney's most scenic Sunday markets
  • Artisan stalls, fresh produce, food vendors and handmade goods
  • Live music in a beautiful Little Bayside Park harbour setting
  • Manly harbour foreshore right alongside — great for a post-market walk
  • Manly Beach just a short walk away for an ocean swim
  • The Corso with cafés and restaurants for a family breakfast or lunch
  • Ferry from Circular Quay makes this a fun family harbour crossing adventure
