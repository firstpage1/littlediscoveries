Bondi Outdoor Cinema: Minions & Monsters

Make a beachside evening of Minions & Monsters at Bondi Pavilion’s free outdoor cinema.

About this activity

Dolphin Court becomes an open-air cinema for this Friday-night family screening. The programme includes free popcorn as well as the film.

Why it’s worth going

  • See Minions & Monsters outdoors.
  • Enjoy the Bondi Pavilion setting.
  • Screening and popcorn are free.

Good to know

The organiser lists a PG classification; this is parental guidance, not a numerical age recommendation. No reservation requirement is stated on the public programme.

Planning your visit

Go to Dolphin Court at Bondi Pavilion, Bondi Beach. The advertised screening window is 7:30pm–9:30pm.
