## Source priorities and verification

Use directories, blogs, community portals and Eventbrite/Humanitix for **discovery**, but confirm every selected event on the current organiser or authorised primary ticket page. Record exact date, times, cost, booking, suitable ages, venue, event-specific original image and attribution. A site listing “free” may hide child tickets or paid sessions; reject if not genuinely free. A timed programme may contain paid and free sessions; choose only specifically verified free events.

Prioritise distinctive/unique experiences, then one-off family-friendly sessions, and recurring activities only as lower-ranked coverage. Never use “searched websites” as evidence that all individual candidate details or images have been verified.
