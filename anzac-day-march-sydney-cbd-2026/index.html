About this activity

The ANZAC Day March is one of Sydney's most impressive annual spectacles — more than 10,000 current and former members of the Australian Defence Force marching in formation down Elizabeth Street to Hyde Park, accompanied by military bands and cheered on by a city full of grateful spectators.

On Saturday 25 April 2026, the March steps off at 9am. Families line the footpaths along Elizabeth Street from as early as 8am to secure a spot. Seeing veterans carry their medals, watching the precision and pride of serving members, and hearing the bands — it is a genuinely moving experience that brings history to life for children far better than any classroom ever could.

Families can look forward to:

  • Over 10,000 marchers including veterans of multiple conflicts and current ADF members
  • Military marching bands playing classic service tunes along the route
  • A powerful, emotional atmosphere that connects children to history in a real way
  • Free to watch from the footpath along Elizabeth Street — no tickets required
  • March concludes at the ANZAC Memorial in Hyde Park South for a Commemoration Service at 12:30pm
  • Easy access by train to Museum, Town Hall or Central stations
  • Broadcast live on ABC TV and iView for those watching from home
