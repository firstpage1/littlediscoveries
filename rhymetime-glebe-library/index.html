Rhymetime at Glebe Library is a free weekly activity for babies and toddlers aged 0 to 2 years and their carers, held at Glebe Library, 186-194 Glebe Point Road, Glebe NSW 2037, in the Inner West of Sydney. Sessions run on weekdays during school term at 10:30am for 20 minutes. Cost: Free. No bookings required. This is a recurring, drop-in program featuring singing, fingerplay and rhymes to develop early literacy skills and social interaction.

About this activity

Rhymetime at Glebe Library is a free weekly session designed especially for babies and toddlers aged 0-2 years and their carers. The 20-minute program fosters a love of reading and helps develop important early literacy skills and social interaction. Sessions start promptly at 10:30am during school term - no bookings required, just arrive a little early.

  • Free entry - no bookings required
  • Builds early literacy skills through singing and fingerplay
  • A wonderful way for babies and toddlers to socialise
  • Runs weekly during school term (no sessions during NSW school or public holidays)
  • Sessions start promptly at 10:30am - please allow time for arrival
