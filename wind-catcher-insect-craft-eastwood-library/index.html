Wind Catcher Insect Craft – Eastwood Library

Make your own bug wind mobile to hang in your window or garden at Eastwood Library. A free school holiday craft for kids aged 7+. Bookings essential; parents must accompany children.

🕐 Time
2:30pm – 3:30pm
💰 Cost
Free free, bookings essential
📍 Venue
Eastwood Library
🧒 Ages
School Kids (5–13 years)
📆 Dates
15 April 2026

Get crafty at Eastwood Library and make your very own bug wind mobile to take home!

Kids aged 7 and over construct a colourful windcatcher insect from craft materials — perfect to hang in a bedroom window or out in the garden where it'll spin and flutter in the breeze.

Part of the City of Ryde's free school holiday program. Children aged 12 and under must be accompanied by a parent or carer for the full session.

Tickets are required for the child only (not the accompanying adult). Bookings essential — register on Eventbrite.
