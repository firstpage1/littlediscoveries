Winnie-the-Pooh Sensory Storytime at Waverley Library

A special free storytime celebrating 100 years of Winnie-the-Pooh, with stories, craft, slime and hands-on sensory play for children aged 3–5.

About this activity

Waverley Library is marking 100 years of Winnie-the-Pooh with a special free sensory storytime on Wednesday 20 May from 10:30am. It's designed for children aged 3–5 and combines picture book stories with hands-on sensory play — including craft and slime.

The session is part of Waverley Library's popular children's program, with the Winnie-the-Pooh theme giving it a special, one-off feel. It's a lovely midweek activity before Vivid Sydney kicks off on Friday.

Bookings are required and spaces are limited.

Why it's worth going

  • It's a one-off centenary event rather than an ordinary weekly storytime.
  • Stories are combined with sensory play, craft and slime, so children can participate rather than just sit and listen.
  • The session is specifically designed for preschoolers aged 3–5.
  • It's free and only 30 minutes long — an easy midweek outing without a big commitment.
  • Waverley Library's established children's program makes it a familiar, parent-friendly setting.

What's included

  • Winnie-the-Pooh themed stories and read-alouds
  • Hands-on craft and sensory play activities
  • Slime making
  • One-off special event celebrating 100 years of Winnie-the-Pooh
  • Ages 3–5 years

Good to know

The session runs from 10:30am to 11:00am at Waverley Library in Bondi Junction. It is free, but bookings are essential. Because it is a special one-off event and spaces are limited, it is worth reserving a place before making the trip.
