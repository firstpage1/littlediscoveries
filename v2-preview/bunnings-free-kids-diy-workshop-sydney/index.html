Why we like it

Not every great family activity needs to be planned weeks in advance. Bunnings' free kids DIY workshops run every weekend at stores across Sydney, making them one of the easiest last-minute family outings to keep in your back pocket.

Each week brings a new project — from building a mini toolbox or planter box to painting a keepsake, making a mosaic or constructing a toy truck — and kids get to take home what they make.

What families can expect

  • A free hands-on DIY project every Saturday and Sunday at Sydney Bunnings stores.
  • A different theme each week, including toolboxes, planters, mosaics, paintings and toy vehicles.
  • Projects designed for children aged 3–12, with a parent or guardian alongside.
  • Sessions usually take around 30–45 minutes.
  • Tools and materials are supplied, and children take their project home.
  • Multiple Sydney locations, so it works well as an easy local weekend option.

Good to know before you go

Children must be accompanied by a parent or guardian. Projects and session times vary by store, so check the official Bunnings listing or your local store before heading out. Some locations accept walk-ins, while others may offer bookings.
