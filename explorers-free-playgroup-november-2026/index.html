About this activity

North Sydney Community Centre celebrates World Children’s Day with a free playgroup session. Its regular Explorers programme is for children under five.

Why it’s worth going

  • Enjoy painting and playdough.
  • Join storytime.
  • Discover the pirate and ocean theme.

Good to know

This dated session is free and online booking is essential. The under-five guidance comes from the council’s Explorers programme description.

Planning your visit

The session runs from 9:30am to noon at North Sydney Community Centre, 220 Miller Street, North Sydney.
