Editor's Choice

Le Marché French Market – Moore Park

Take the family on a mini trip to France with freshly baked croissants, crêpes, macarons, raclette, French music and more than 40 market stalls. Free entry; food and shopping extra.

What makes it special

Stroll beneath the covered market canopy at The Entertainment Quarter for a French-themed Sunday outing. Browse more than 40 stalls selling pastries, macarons, crêpes, baguettes, cheeses and artisan gifts, with live jazz adding a Parisian atmosphere. Children can enjoy the bustling stalls and treats. Entry is free, while food and purchases cost extra.

  • Food: fresh croissants, macarons, crêpes, raclette, baguettes and other French favourites
  • Market atmosphere: over 40 gourmet and artisan stalls, with live jazz
  • With children: a relaxed opportunity to explore French flavours and culture together

Before you go

The market is fully undercover and dog-friendly, with two hours of free parking advertised by the organiser. Food and shopping are optional purchases. Check the official listing for any last-minute changes.
