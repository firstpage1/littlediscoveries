Coral Sea Park in Randwick transforms into a free outdoor party for Youth Week on Wednesday 24 April — and the whole family is welcome. Randwick City Council's South East Block Party brings together live entertainment, free food, fun activities and games in one of the Eastern Suburbs' best parks, with harbour and ocean views as the backdrop.

Youth Week is a NSW-wide annual celebration of young people aged 12–25, and the South East Block Party is one of the most relaxed and welcoming local events of the year. Older kids can connect with community services and creative workshops, while families with younger children can enjoy the park atmosphere, live music and free activities. It's the kind of event where you wander in not knowing what to expect and end up staying for hours.

Families can look forward to:

  • Free entry, free food and free entertainment — bring nothing but yourselves
  • Live music and performances throughout the afternoon
  • Fun activities, games and interactive zones for all ages
  • Beautiful Coral Sea Park setting with harbour views in Randwick
  • Part of NSW Youth Week — a welcoming community celebration
  • Easy to combine with a walk along the Coogee to Bondi coastal track
  • Parking at Coral Sea Park or accessible by bus from the city

Free — Just Show Up
