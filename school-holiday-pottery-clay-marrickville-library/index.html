School Holiday: Kids' Pottery & Clay Workshop – Marrickville Library

Kids get their hands muddy creating their own clay animals and mini sculptures in this free school holiday workshop at Marrickville Library.

Inner West Council's school holiday pottery workshop lets kids aged 6–12 get creative with clay — no experience needed. A professional ceramic artist guides children through making their own animal sculptures and pinch pots to take home.

What's included:

  • Ages 6–12 years
  • Free — bookings essential
  • All materials provided
  • Take home your finished piece
  • Multiple sessions available — check website for times
