About this activity

Grab a picnic rug and head to Bondi Beach South for Bondi Firelight — a magical free community evening hosted by Waverley Council.

From 5:45pm, enjoy:

  • A roaring community bonfire on the sand
  • Fire twirlers and live chill music
  • Toasted marshmallows
  • Roving entertainment
  • A relaxed, alcohol-free atmosphere

Families are encouraged to bring a picnic rug, low chair, and musical instruments. No bookings required — just show up and soak in the Bondi atmosphere. One of the most iconic free family nights Sydney has to offer.
