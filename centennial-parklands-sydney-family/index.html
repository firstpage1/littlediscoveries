About this activity

Centennial Parklands is one of Sydney's most beloved green spaces, covering 580 acres right in the heart of the Eastern Suburbs. It's a perfect free day out for families at any time of year.

Things to do:

  • Visit the duck and goose ponds
  • Free playgrounds for different age groups
  • Hire bikes and pedal cars (small fee)
  • Wide open lawns for a picnic
  • Birdwatching and nature walks
  • Seasonal events including Easter egg hunts and summer concerts
  • Horse riding nearby (paid)
