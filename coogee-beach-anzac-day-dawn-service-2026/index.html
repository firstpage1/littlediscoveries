While thousands gather at Martin Place for the main Sydney Dawn Service, the Coogee community gathers on its own beach at dawn on ANZAC Day for a service that is quieter, more intimate, and no less moving. With the Pacific Ocean stretching out behind the memorial and the early light catching the waves, it's a genuinely special way to mark the day — and a manageable experience for families with children.

Coogee Beach has long been a gathering place for the Eastern Suburbs community on ANZAC Day. The atmosphere is respectful and warm, with locals of all ages coming together to remember. For families, it's a wonderful opportunity to explain the significance of ANZAC Day in a setting that feels personal rather than overwhelming — and the beach is right there for a walk afterwards.

Families can look forward to:

  • A free, community-run ANZAC Day dawn service right on the beach
  • Beautiful ocean backdrop as the sun rises over the Pacific
  • Intimate local atmosphere — quieter and more accessible than city services
  • Easy to explain to children — a meaningful first ANZAC Day experience
  • Coogee Beach right there for a family walk or play after the service
  • Close to Coogee Bay Road cafés for a post-service family breakfast
  • Well-served by buses from Bondi Junction and the city

Free — Just Show Up
