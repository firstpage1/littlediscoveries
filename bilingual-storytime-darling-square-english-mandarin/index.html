About this activity

Bilingual Storytime at Darling Square Library is a free weekly session for pre-schoolers aged 3-5 years and their carers. Stories, songs and rhymes are delivered in both English and Mandarin, supporting early literacy development, nurturing Mandarin language knowledge, and providing a wonderful cultural exchange for the whole family. Sessions start promptly at 11:30am and run for 30 minutes during school term. No bookings required.

  • Free entry - no bookings required
  • Stories, songs and rhymes in both English and Mandarin
  • Supports early literacy and Mandarin language development
  • A wonderful cultural exchange for the whole family
  • Weekly during school term - sessions start promptly at 11:30am
