Sydney Zoo – Western Sydney's Wild Adventure

Come face to face with giraffes, gorillas, hippos and hundreds more animals at Sydney Zoo in Bungarribee Park — a brand new world-class zoo in Western Sydney.

🕐 Hours
9:30am - 5:00pm
💰 Cost
From $27 per child
📍 Venue
Sydney Zoo
🧒 Ages
All ages welcome

Sydney Zoo opened in 2019 in Bungarribee Park, Blacktown, and is Western Sydney's first major zoo. It's a stunning, purpose-built facility where animals live in large, naturalistic habitats.

What you'll see:

  • Giraffes and African savannah animals
  • Western lowland gorillas
  • Hippos and crocodiles
  • Australian wildlife including koalas and wombats
  • Over 100 species in total

Good to know:

  • Located next to Eastern Creek Raceway — easy to find
  • Plenty of parking
  • Cafe and picnic areas on site
  • Great value family tickets available
