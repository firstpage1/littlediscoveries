Pyrmont Food & Wine Festival – Free Kids Zone at Pirrama Park

Free entry festival at Pirrama Park, Pyrmont with a dedicated Kids Zone — jumping castle, face painting, mini golf, live music and loads of food. Saturday 23 and Sunday 24 May.

The Pyrmont Food & Wine Festival takes over Pirrama Park on the Pyrmont waterfront this Saturday 23 and Sunday 24 May, with free entry and a packed Kids Zone that'll keep little ones entertained all afternoon.

The Kids Zone features a jumping castle, face painting, mini golf (Putt Party Hire), laughing clowns and a chill-out parents zone right next door so you can keep an eye on the kids while you enjoy the festival atmosphere. There's also live music across two stages and a great lineup of food and wine vendors.

Pirrama Park is right on the Pyrmont waterfront with beautiful harbour views — a lovely spot for a weekend family outing. Free entry, no tickets needed, just show up.

Kids Zone highlights:

  • Jumping castle and laughing clowns
  • Face painting
  • Mini golf with Putt Party Hire
  • Live music across two stages
  • Parents chill zone adjacent to Kids Zone
  • Free entry both Saturday 23 and Sunday 24 May, 11am–5pm
  • Pirrama Park on the Pyrmont waterfront — stunning harbour setting

Head to Pirrama Park This Weekend

Free entry — no tickets needed. Open both Saturday and Sunday 11am–5pm.
