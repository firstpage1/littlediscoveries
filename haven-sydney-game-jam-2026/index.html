About this activity

Haven combines workshops and independent building time with other teen creators. Beginners can learn alongside experienced coders, then share their finished games in Sunday’s arcade and voting session.

Why it’s worth going

  • Learn through Godot and Ren’Py workshops.
  • Build a game with other teenagers.
  • Food and snacks are included.

Good to know

Sign up with the organiser. This is a daytime event across two days, 9am–6pm each day. The image shows a related Hack Club programme, rather than this future Sydney event.

Planning your visit

La Trobe University Sydney Campus is at 255 Elizabeth Street, Sydney. Saturday starts with registration at 9am; workshops follow the opening ceremony.
