Featherdale Wildlife Park

Get nose-to-nose with wombats, hand-feed kangaroos and spot koalas up close at one of Sydney's best wildlife parks — a guaranteed hit with kids of every age.

🕐 Hours
9:00am - 5:00pm
💰 Cost
From $15 per child
📍 Venue
Featherdale Wildlife Park
🧒 Ages
All ages welcome

Featherdale Wildlife Park in Doonside is one of the best places in Sydney to get up close with Australian animals. With over 1,700 animals across 260 species, it's a hands-on wildlife experience the whole family will love.

Highlights include:

  • Hand-feed kangaroos and wallabies
  • See koalas, wombats and Tasmanian devils
  • Meet reptiles and birds in walk-through aviaries
  • Up-close encounters with native Australian wildlife
  • Perfect for all ages including toddlers and babies
