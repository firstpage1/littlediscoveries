📖 Storytime 🌟 Ages 3–5 🏙️ Eastern Suburbs ✅ Free

Tales for Tykes is Waverley Library's free weekly storytime for pre-schoolers aged 3–5, running every Tuesday during school terms at Bondi Junction. Two sessions are available — 10:00am and 11:00am — so you can pick whichever suits your morning.

Each session combines picture book read-alouds, action songs and a simple craft activity, all designed to build early literacy skills and a love of books in a fun, social setting.

Waverley Library is conveniently located inside the Bondi Junction area near public transport. Bookings are required and fill up fast — check the library website to reserve a spot.

What each session includes:

  • Free weekly storytime every Tuesday during school terms
  • Two sessions — 10:00am and 11:00am — choose which suits you
  • Stories, rhymes and a craft activity each week
  • Ages 3–5 years
  • Bookings required — register via Waverley Library website
  • Waverley Library, 32–48 Denison St, Bondi Junction
