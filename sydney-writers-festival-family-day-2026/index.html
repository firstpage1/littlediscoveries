The Sydney Writers' Festival Family Day is one of the best free days out for families in Sydney — a full day of books, creativity and fun at the stunning Carriageworks precinct in Eveleigh.

Free drop-in activities include:

  • Russ the Story Bus — A mobile library bus packed with books, open all day to browse. Free, no bookings needed.
  • Where's Wally? Activity Station — Free treasure hunt and a giant colouring wall.
  • Story Factory: Plant-a-Poem — Write a poem and plant native seeds to take home.
  • Live Storytimes — Free sessions with authors including First Nations storytelling, picture books and interactive stories.
  • The Epic Illustrator Battle — Watch illustrators Sami Bayly, Mick Elliott, Gabriel Evans and Sandra Steffensen compete live.
  • Kids' Dance Party — Free dance party at 1pm.
  • Cartoon Portraits — Author/illustrator Mick Elliott draws live cartoon portraits of audience members.
  • Badge-making and craft stations — Creative hands-on activities throughout the day.

Good to know

Most drop-in activities are completely free. A small number of premium sessions are ticketed (from $29 per child). Carriageworks is easily accessible by train — Redfern Station is a 10-minute walk. Arrive early for the most popular storytimes. Suitable for ages 3 and up; toddlers welcome with an adult.
